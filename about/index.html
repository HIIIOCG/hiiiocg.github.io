About HIIIO Digital.

VTuber 3D model studio · Taipei, Taiwan

HIIIO Digital (合成香料動畫有限公司, also known as HIIIO, HiiioCG or HIIIO CG) is a VTuber 3D character modeling studio based in Taipei, Taiwan. We turn 2D character designs into complete, ready-to-use 3D models.

We offer the best value for money on the market for VTuber 3D models.

We have worked on 3D projects for over 50 Taiwanese VTubers, and we also make 3D characters for Taiwanese corporate VTuber agencies as an uncredited subcontractor.

FAQ

How long does a VTuber 3D model take?

It depends on the workflow: with our AI process (AI-assisted modeling, then hand-finished by our team to match your design) a character takes about 3–4 days; fully hand-made modeling takes about 1 month.

How much does a VTuber 3D model cost?

We offer the best value for money on the market for VTuber 3D models. The exact quote depends on your character and needs; send us your design for a free 3D preview and an exact quote.

What do I need to provide?

A single front view of your character is enough; a three-view sheet is welcome if you have one. We first make a free 3D preview from your design, then send the scope, an exact quote and available dates.

What equipment do I need to stream with a 3D model?

A webcam on your computer, or a phone connected to your computer over Wi-Fi.

How do I contact you?

Email studio@hiiiodigital.com with your character design.

常見問題

製作一個 VTuber 3D 模型需要多久？

依製作方式而定：採用 AI 製程（AI 輔助建模，再由團隊依原設定圖人工修整）約 3–4 天可完成一個角色；全人工製作約需 1 個月。

VTuber 3D 模型的價格是多少？

我們是市場上 CP 值最高的 VTuber 3D 模型廠商。實際報價依角色與需求而定，寄設定圖給我們即可取得免費 3D 預覽與確切報價。

委託 3D 化需要準備什麼？

只需要一張角色正視圖；若已有三視圖也歡迎提供。我們會先依設定圖製作免費的 3D 模擬預覽，再提供製作內容、確切報價與可安排的檔期，供你評估。

用 3D 模型直播需要什麼設備？

只需要在電腦上接一台網路攝影機，或讓手機透過 Wi-Fi 連上電腦即可。

如何聯絡？

請將角色設定圖寄到 studio@hiiiodigital.com。
