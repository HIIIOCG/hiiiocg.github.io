Privacy Policy.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

HIIIO Digital (合成香料動畫有限公司, “we”, “us” or “the Company”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, process and use personal data when you visit our website hiiiodigital.com (the “Website”), contact us by email, or use our software products. It is drafted in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act of Taiwan (R.O.C.).

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Website, our email contact channels, and the software products we publish, including our planned desktop 3D character engine and iPhone face capture app (together, the “Products”). Where a specific Product handles data differently from this policy, the difference will be described on that Product’s page, inside the app, or in a supplementary notice. If a supplementary notice conflicts with this policy, the supplementary notice prevails for that Product.

2. The Website

The Website is a static information site. It has no user accounts, no forms, and does not use cookies or any analytics or advertising trackers. However, when you visit it, the following third-party services technically receive connection information:

  • Hosting and network services. The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and domain and network services are provided by Cloudflare. To deliver pages and keep them secure and reliable, these providers may process connection logs such as your IP address, browser type and request time under their own privacy policies. We do not obtain or use these logs to identify you.
  • Web fonts. The Website loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers. Google may receive connection information such as your IP address and process it under Google’s privacy policy.

The Website links to external sites such as Facebook and X (Twitter). Those sites are responsible for their own data practices, and this policy does not apply to them.

3. Contacting us by email

When you email us (for example at studio@hiiiodigital.com or support@hiiiodigital.com), we receive your email address, your name or signature, and any information you choose to include in the message and attachments.

  • Purpose: to answer your enquiry, provide support, handle business or production requests, and respond to your personal data requests.
  • Processing: our company mailboxes are operated through email service providers (currently Cloudflare Email Routing forwarding to Google Gmail, with outgoing mail sent via Resend). These providers process data only to deliver and store email.
  • Retention: we keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and delete it once it is no longer needed or any legally required retention period has ended.
  • We do not sell or rent your contact details, and we do not send you marketing email without your consent.

4. Our software Products

Our Products are still in development and have not yet been released. They are designed according to the following principles:

  • Local processing first. The core features of our Products run on your own devices. We do not need, and do not intend, to upload your content to our servers in order to provide those features.
  • Face capture data stays on your network. The planned design of our iPhone face capture app is that face tracking data captured on your iPhone (such as numerical facial expression and head movement values) is sent only over your own local network to your own computer, so that your 3D character can use it in real time. This data is not sent to us, and we do not store, analyse or use it to identify you.
  • Camera and network permissions. The face capture app needs access to the camera (including face tracking) and to your local network in order to work. Your device will ask for your permission before use, and you can turn these permissions off at any time in your device settings.
  • Other data. If a Product later needs to process data not described in this policy (for example accounts, payments, crash reports or usage statistics), we will describe what is collected, why, and your choices before that Product is released, and obtain your consent where required.

If you download or purchase a Product through a third-party platform such as the Apple App Store, the platform processes your account and transaction data under its own privacy policy. We normally receive only aggregated sales reports from the platform.

5. Sharing

Apart from the service providers described above that we need in order to operate, we do not share your personal data with third parties, except with your consent, where required by law or by a court or competent authority, or where necessary to protect the rights and safety of the Company, our users or the public.

6. International transfers

The service providers mentioned above (such as GitHub, Cloudflare, Google and Resend) may operate servers outside Taiwan, so your data may be processed in other countries or regions.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, such as restricting mailbox access and enabling account security verification. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information by email.

8. Your rights

Under Article 3 of Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act, you may, regarding personal data we hold about you: make an inquiry or request to review it, request a copy, request supplementation or correction, request that we stop collecting, processing or using it, and request its deletion. To make a request, email support@hiiiodigital.com. We may need to verify your identity first. If you are located in another country or region, you may have similar rights under local law; please contact us in the same way.

9. Children

The Website and Products are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have received a child’s data by mistake, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy due to changes in law or in our services. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page, and we will give prominent notice on the Website or in the Product for material changes.

隱私權政策.

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 25 日

合成香料動畫有限公司（HIIIO Digital，以下稱「本公司」或「我們」）重視你的隱私。本政策說明我們在你使用官方網站 hiiiodigital.com（以下稱「本網站」）、透過電子郵件與我們聯絡，以及使用本公司軟體產品時，如何蒐集、處理及利用你的個人資料。本政策依中華民國（台灣）《個人資料保護法》訂定。

1. 適用範圍

本政策適用於本網站、本公司的電子郵件聯絡管道，以及本公司發行的軟體產品，包括規劃中的桌面 3D 角色引擎與 iPhone 臉部捕捉 App（以下合稱「產品」）。個別產品若有與本政策不同的資料處理方式，會在該產品的說明頁面、App 內或另行公告的補充說明中載明；補充說明與本政策不一致時，以補充說明為準。

2. 本網站

本網站是靜態的介紹網站，沒有會員帳號、沒有表單，也不使用 Cookie 或任何流量分析、廣告追蹤工具。不過，在你瀏覽本網站時，以下第三方服務會在技術上接觸到你的連線資訊：

  • 網站代管與網路服務：本網站由 GitHub Pages 代管，網域解析等網路服務由 Cloudflare 提供。這些服務供應商為了傳送網頁、維持安全與穩定，可能會依其各自的隱私權政策處理你的 IP 位址、瀏覽器類型、請求時間等連線紀錄。本公司不會取得或使用這些紀錄來辨識你。
  • 網頁字型：本網站從 Google Fonts 載入字型，因此你的瀏覽器會連線到 Google 的伺服器，Google 可能因此取得你的 IP 位址等連線資訊，並依 Google 的隱私權政策處理。

本網站含有連到 Facebook、X（Twitter）等外部網站的連結。你點擊後前往的網站，其資料處理由該網站自行負責，不適用本政策。

3. 透過電子郵件聯絡我們

當你寄信給我們（例如 studio@hiiiodigital.com 或 support@hiiiodigital.com），我們會取得你的電子郵件地址、姓名或署名，以及你在信件內容與附件中主動提供的資訊。

  • 利用目的：回覆你的詢問、提供客服、處理合作或委託事宜，以及處理你的個人資料請求。
  • 處理方式：公司信箱的收發透過電子郵件服務供應商處理（目前為 Cloudflare Email Routing 轉寄至 Google Gmail，寄信經由 Resend）。這些供應商僅為傳遞與保存郵件而處理資料。
  • 保存期間：在處理你的詢問所需的期間內保存，並在法令要求的保存期間屆滿或不再需要後刪除。
  • 我們不會將你的聯絡資料出售或出租給任何人，也不會在未經你同意的情況下寄送行銷郵件。

4. 本公司的軟體產品

本公司的產品目前仍在開發中，尚未正式上線。產品設計遵循以下原則：

  • 以本機處理為原則：產品的主要功能在你自己的裝置上運作，我們不需要、也不打算為了提供核心功能而把你的內容上傳到本公司的伺服器。
  • 臉部捕捉資料留在你的網路內：iPhone 臉部捕捉 App 規劃的設計，是讓 iPhone 擷取的臉部追蹤資料（例如臉部表情與頭部動作的數值），只透過你自己的區域網路傳送到你自己的電腦，供你的 3D 角色即時使用。這些資料不會傳送到本公司，本公司也不會保存、分析或用於辨識你的身分。
  • 相機與網路權限：臉部捕捉 App 需要使用相機（含臉部追蹤功能）與區域網路的權限才能運作。系統會在使用前向你請求授權，你可以隨時在裝置設定中關閉。
  • 其他資料：若某項產品日後需要處理本政策未提及的資料（例如帳號、付款、當機報告或使用統計），我們會在該產品上線前另行說明蒐集的資料、目的與你的選擇，並在需要時取得你的同意。

透過 Apple App Store 等第三方平台下載或購買產品時，平台業者會依其自身的隱私權政策處理你的帳號與交易資料，本公司通常只會收到平台提供的彙總銷售報表。

5. 資料的分享

除前述為提供服務所需的服務供應商外，我們不會將你的個人資料提供給第三人，但以下情形除外：取得你的同意、依法令規定或司法機關、主管機關的要求，或為保護本公司、使用者或公眾的權利與安全所必要。

6. 跨境傳輸

前述服務供應商（如 GitHub、Cloudflare、Google、Resend）的伺服器可能位於台灣以外的國家或地區，你的資料可能因此在境外被處理。

7. 資料安全

我們採取合理的技術與管理措施保護你的個人資料，例如限制信箱的存取權限並啟用帳號安全驗證。但網際網路傳輸無法保證絕對安全，請避免在信件中提供不必要的敏感資料。

8. 你的權利

依《個人資料保護法》第 3 條，你可以就本公司保有的你的個人資料行使以下權利：查詢或請求閱覽、請求製給複製本、請求補充或更正、請求停止蒐集、處理或利用，以及請求刪除。請寄信至 support@hiiiodigital.com 提出，我們可能需要先確認你的身分。若你位於其他國家或地區，也可以依當地法律享有相應的權利，請同樣與我們聯絡。

9. 兒童隱私

本網站與產品並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，我們不會在知情的情況下蒐集兒童的個人資料。若你認為我們誤收了兒童的資料，請與我們聯絡，我們會盡速刪除。

10. 政策的修訂

我們可能因法令或服務內容變更而修訂本政策，修訂後會更新本頁面上方的「最後更新」日期；若有重大變更，會在本網站或產品內以明顯方式公告。
