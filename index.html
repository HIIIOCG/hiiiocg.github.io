HIIIO Digital — 3D Character Modeling Studio in Taiwan

HIIIO Digital (also known as HIIIO, HiiioCG or HIIIO CG; 合成香料動畫有限公司) is a 3D CG studio based in Taipei, Taiwan.

We make complete, ready-to-use VTuber 3D models from 2D character designs, and have worked on 3D projects for over 50 Taiwanese VTubers — the best value for money on the market.

HIIIO is a group of outstanding 3D Generalists. We transform all types of images into 3D models and produce the best quality works.

HIIIO extends the potential of 2D pictures with the power of 3D technologies. Our innovative modeling and hand-crafted cell shading techniques recreate the original illustration in the virtual world with the highest fidelity.
